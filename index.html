Frequently asked questions

Clearing WhatsApp storage, finding duplicate photos, and how Gleamo keeps your data private.

How do I clear WhatsApp storage on Android?

WhatsApp saves every forwarded image, meme, sticker and video to your phone, and it builds up fast. In Gleamo, open the WhatsApp media tool to see those files grouped together, review them, and delete the ones you no longer need. Because you confirm the deletion yourself, your saved chats and important media stay safe.

How do I find and delete duplicate photos?

Duplicate photos usually come from sharing, downloading the same image twice, or burst shots. Gleamo scans your gallery, groups duplicate and near-duplicate photos together, and lets you keep one copy and delete the rest in a couple of taps — so you recover space without losing the picture.

Is Gleamo really free? Are there ads or subscriptions?

Yes. Gleamo is free with no ads, no subscription, no paywall and no in-app purchases. Every cleaning tool is available to everyone at no cost. Many popular cleaner apps charge for the same features or fill the screen with ads; Gleamo does not.

Does Gleamo send my photos or data anywhere?

No. Gleamo does not request the internet permission at all, so the app physically cannot upload your photos, files or any data off your phone. Everything is scanned and cleaned locally on your device, which makes Gleamo private by design.

What is a good free cleaner app for Android without ads?

Gleamo is a free Android cleaner built around no ads, no subscription and no internet permission. It finds duplicate photos, WhatsApp media, large files and junk, shows a clear storage overview, and only deletes what you confirm. It is a good fit if you want to free up space without ads, trial billing, or your files leaving the phone.

Does Gleamo clear app cache?

Gleamo helps you clear recoverable junk and cache using Android's own storage tools. Since Android 6, apps are not allowed to silently wipe other apps' caches, so Gleamo is honest about what it can clear rather than promising to erase all cache. It focuses on the space it can genuinely free: duplicates, large files, WhatsApp media and accessible junk.

Can Gleamo hide private photos and videos?

Yes. Secret Space is a private vault inside Gleamo where you move photos and videos so other apps cannot see them. It is locked by your phone's own screen lock — PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint — and Gleamo never sees or stores your lock. The files stay on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Can Gleamo compress videos to save space?

Yes. Gleamo can compress large videos into smaller copies saved to your gallery, so you keep the memory while using far less storage. The original is only removed if you confirm it.

Will Gleamo delete something I need?

No file is deleted automatically. Gleamo shows you what it found, you select what to remove, and you confirm before anything is deleted. This keeps you in control and prevents accidental loss of photos or files you want to keep.
